The Club Andino of Punta Arenas is the reference center for mountain and snow sports enthusiasts in the Magallanes region.

Located a few kilometers from downtown, the club offers ski and snowboard runs for all levels, as well as hiking and mountaineering routes.

During the austral winter, the club becomes the epicenter of winter activity, with competitions, classes, and equipment rental.

In the summer season, the slopes transform into trekking routes with privileged views of the strait and the mountain range.
