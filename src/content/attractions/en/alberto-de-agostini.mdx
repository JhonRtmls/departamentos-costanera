Alberto de Agostini National Park is a vast protected territory located in the Darwin mountain range in Tierra del Fuego, accessible mainly by sea.

Named after the Italian missionary and explorer Alberto de Agostini, the park houses breathtaking glaciers, deep fjords, and ancient forests.

It is one of the preferred destinations for navigation among the ice, with excursions that allow close observation of glaciers and marine fauna.

The park offers a unique remote exploration experience, ideal for adventurous travelers seeking pristine landscapes.
