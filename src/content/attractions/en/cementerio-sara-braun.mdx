The Sara Braun Municipal Cemetery is internationally recognized as one of the most beautiful cemeteries in the world, comparable to the Recoleta Cemetery in Buenos Aires.

Its walkways are flanked by impressive marble and granite mausoleums belonging to the most powerful families in the region, decorated with sculptures and architectural details of great artistic value.

The cypress trees carved into shapes that evoke regional culture and history are one of the hallmarks of the place, creating a unique atmosphere of respect and beauty.

The cemetery is also a reflection of the history of Punta Arenas, with graves that tell the stories of immigrants, pioneers, and key figures in the development of Patagonia.
