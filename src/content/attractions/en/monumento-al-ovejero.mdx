The Monument to the Shepherd is a sculptural tribute to the workers of Patagonia, located on Avenida Bulnes in Punta Arenas.

Composed of twelve life-sized figures, the sculpture represents the different trades that forged the identity of the region: shepherds, sheep shearers, riders, and other rural tasks of the southern countryside.

Inaugurated in 2016, the monument has become a must-see landmark for visitors to the city, offering a unique photo opportunity and a window into the rural history of Magallanes.

The figures are arranged in a landscape that evokes the Patagonian steppe, creating an immersive experience that connects visitors with the pioneering spirit of the region.
