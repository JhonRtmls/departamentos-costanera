The Heritage District of Punta Arenas is a walk through the streets of the historic center, where European-style mansions and colorful facades tell the story of the immigration that forged the city.

Magallanes, Bories, and Lautaro Navarro streets concentrate the largest number of heritage buildings, with their characteristic zinc and wood cladding.

Many of these buildings have been restored and today house museums, art galleries, restaurants, and cafes that invite exploration.

The pedestrian route is accessible and signposted, allowing visitors to discover the architectural details and stories of each building through information panels.
