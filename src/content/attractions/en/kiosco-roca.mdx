Kiosco Roca is one of the most beloved icons of the Punta Arenas waterfront, a traditional heritage kiosk dating from the early 20th century.

Declared a National Monument, this small eclectic-style building has become an unmissable meeting point for locals and tourists alike.

Located steps from the Strait of Magellan, the kiosk offers a privileged sea view and is the perfect place to enjoy a coffee or ice cream while contemplating the landscape.

Its heritage value and strategic location make it a mandatory stop on any walk along the waterfront.
