Parque del Estrecho (Strait Park) is a historic park located 60 km south of Punta Arenas, housing Fuerte Bulnes, the first Chilean settlement on the Strait of Magellan, founded in 1843.

The park recreates 19th-century life with reconstructions of historic buildings, including the fort, chapel, dwellings, and the period store.

Visitors can walk a coastal trail with spectacular views of the strait and visit the interpretation center.

The park also has picnic areas, viewpoints, and a replica of the historic lighthouse.
