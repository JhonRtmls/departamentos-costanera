The Nao Victoria Museum displays full-scale replicas of the most important ships in the navigation history of the Strait of Magellan.

Highlights include the replica of the Nao Victoria, the first ship to circumnavigate the world (1519-1522), and HMS Beagle, famous for carrying Charles Darwin during his expedition that revolutionized science.

Located on the shores of the Strait of Magellan, the museum allows visitors to board these vessels and experience first-hand the navigation conditions of the era.

The museum features expert guides who narrate the stories of exploration, shipwrecks, and discoveries that marked the maritime history of the region.
