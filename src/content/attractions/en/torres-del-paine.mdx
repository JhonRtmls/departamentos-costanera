Torres del Paine National Park is the crown jewel of Chilean Patagonia and one of the most impressive natural destinations on the planet.

Famous for its imposing granite towers rising more than 2,800 meters above sea level, the park offers landscapes that seem from another world: ancient glaciers, turquoise lakes, powerful rivers, and unique biodiversity.

It is home to species such as the guanaco, Andean condor, puma, and rhea, as well as an incredible variety of southern flora.

Visitors can explore the park through world-famous trekking routes such as the W and the O, or through day excursions to the main viewpoints. From Punta Arenas, access is possible by land (5 hours by car) or by air to Puerto Natales.
