Muñoz Gamero Plaza de Armas is the bustling heart of Punta Arenas, a green space surrounded by the city most emblematic buildings.

At the center stands the iconic Fountain of the Eagles, a monument paying homage to the pioneers of the region.

The plaza is surrounded by the Punta Arenas Cathedral, the Sara Braun Palace, the Government Building, and other European-style heritage buildings.

The plaza is the ideal starting point for exploring the historic center, with its benches, gardens, and constant activity of craft fairs and cultural events.
