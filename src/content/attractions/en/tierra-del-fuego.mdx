Tierra del Fuego is an archipelago located south of the Strait of Magellan, shared between Chile and Argentina, famous for its remote landscapes and fascinating history.

The Chilean side of the archipelago offers ancient forests, glacial lakes, salmon rivers, and a jagged coast full of channels and fjords.

The region has a rich history that includes the indigenous Yámana and Selknam peoples, as well as expeditions by Magellan and Darwin.

Puerto Williams, the southernmost city in the world, is the main access point to the Chilean territory of Tierra del Fuego.
