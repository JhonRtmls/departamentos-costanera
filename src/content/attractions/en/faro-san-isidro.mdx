San Isidro Lighthouse is an emblematic lighthouse located on the northern coast of the Strait of Magellan, known for its isolation and the wild beauty of its surroundings.

Built in 1904, the lighthouse has witnessed countless shipwrecks and rescue operations.

The environment around the lighthouse is a paradise for nature lovers, with lenga forests, jagged coasts, and abundant fauna.

Access is via land excursions from Punta Arenas, combining the visit to the lighthouse with a walk along the coast.
