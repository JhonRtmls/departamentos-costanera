The king penguin colony at Bahía Inútil is one of the most fascinating natural attractions in Chilean Patagonia, located in Tierra del Fuego.

This colony, established in 2010, has grown to become an important tourist destination where visitors can observe these majestic penguins.

Unlike Magellanic penguins, king penguins are larger and more striking, with their characteristic orange and yellow plumage.

Access to the colony is from Punta Arenas via excursions that include crossing the Strait of Magellan by ferry.
