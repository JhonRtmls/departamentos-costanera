Cerro de la Cruz is the most famous natural viewpoint in Punta Arenas, offering a 360-degree panoramic view of the city, the Strait of Magellan, and the Darwin mountain range.

Located just a few blocks from downtown, the climb up the hill is an accessible walk that rewards with one of the most photographed postcards of Chilean Patagonia.

At the top, a large wooden cross gives the place its name, along with benches and rest areas ideal for contemplating the landscape.

It is especially recommended at sunset, when the sun sets over the strait painting the sky in orange and pink tones.
