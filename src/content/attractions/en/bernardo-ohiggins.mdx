Bernardo O'Higgins National Park is the largest national park in Chile, spanning over 3.5 million hectares that embrace the southern Andes mountain range.

The park is home to the imposing Southern Ice Field, the third largest continental ice mass in the world.

Within the park lies the Pío XI Glacier, the largest glacier in the southern hemisphere outside Antarctica, with an ice front over 6 km wide.

Access to the park is exclusively by sea or air, with excursions departing from Puerto Natales or Punta Arenas.
