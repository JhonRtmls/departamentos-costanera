The Magallanes National Reserve is a protected area located just 10 km from Punta Arenas, a true natural sanctuary for trekking and bird watching enthusiasts.

Its trails wind through forests of lenga, coigüe, and ñirre trees, offering direct contact with southern flora and fauna.

The reserve is home to species such as the black woodpecker, chimango, and Patagonian fox, as well as a wide variety of waterfowl in its lagoons.

The main trail, of low to moderate difficulty, leads to natural viewpoints with panoramic views of the Strait of Magellan and the city.
