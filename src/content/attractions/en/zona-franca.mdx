The Zona Franca (Duty Free Zone) of Punta Arenas is the main shopping center in Chilean Patagonia, a commercial complex offering tax-free products of all kinds.

With over 200 stores, visitors can find everything from electronics and clothing to liquors, perfumes, and sporting goods at competitive prices.

The complex also includes a food court, cinemas, and entertainment areas, making it an ideal day-trip destination.

Foreign tourists can access additional tax exemption benefits, making the shopping experience even more attractive.
