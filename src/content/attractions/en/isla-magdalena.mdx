Isla Magdalena National Park, located in the Strait of Magellan, is home to one of the largest penguin colonies in South America, with over 60,000 breeding pairs of Magellanic penguins.

The island is accessible by ferry from Punta Arenas, in a journey of approximately two hours crossing the waters of the strait.

Visitors can walk a marked trail that crosses the penguin colony, closely observing their natural behavior.

In addition to penguins, the island is home to cormorants, seagulls, and sea lions, and has a historic lighthouse dating from 1904.
