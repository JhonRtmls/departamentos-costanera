Cabo de Hornos National Park is the southernmost national park in the world, declared a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in 2005.

Located in the Tierra del Fuego archipelago, the park protects the subantarctic landscapes of Cape Horn, historically famous for being one of the most dangerous navigation points.

Its flora includes dwarf forests of lenga and peat bogs, while its marine fauna includes elephant seals, sea lions, and albatrosses.

Access to the park is via boat excursions from Punta Arenas or Ushuaia.
