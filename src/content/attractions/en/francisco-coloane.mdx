Francisco Coloane Marine Park is a marine sanctuary located in the Strait of Magellan, created to protect the feeding habitat of the humpback whale.

The park is one of the best places in the world for whale watching in their natural habitat, with the observation season running from October to April.

In addition to humpback whales, the area is home to southern dolphins, sea lions, Peale dolphins, and a great diversity of seabirds.

Whale watching excursions depart from Punta Arenas and offer an unforgettable experience sailing through the waters of the strait.
